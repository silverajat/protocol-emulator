What is running

Your program is assembled to 16-bit words and run on one engine of the model, clock by clock: the engine's clock divider, delays, bit timer and pacing, serial unit, stuffer, CRC and FIFOs all behave as on the chip. The chip has three such engines sharing 64 words.

Reading the capture

Each row is a pin over time in clocks; the bottom row is the engine's program counter, with labels where they apply. Words the engine pushed to its RX FIFO are listed with the clock they appeared.

Taking it further

The same program runs on the real thing: sw/pesim.py does this from the command line with a VCD out, and sw/pe.py loads it onto the LED board's FPGA, which runs the chip's exact RTL.
